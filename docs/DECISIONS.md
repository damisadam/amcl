# Decisions and unresolved questions

Each entry records a conflict or gap in the source documents (`AMCL V1.5 MultiTenant Combine Doc.docx`,
Pamex Soft / Al-Mawakhat agreement V1.5), its impact, the **demo behaviour implemented**, and what must be
confirmed before production. Nothing here redefines legal, regulatory or accounting policy — demo behaviour
is configurable and clearly labelled.

| ID | Topic | Source conflict / gap | Demo behaviour | Needs confirmation by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | Wallet safeguarding owner | Wallet doc §2.4/§3.4/§5.4 say funds sit in the **AMCL safeguarded account**; Wallet §1.1 and FMS §5.1/§16 say each **tenant** safeguards its own wallets. | `tenants.wallet_safeguarding_model` (`platform_pooled` for AMCL demo, `tenant_safeguarded` for others). Accounting is identical: each tenant's GL carries a *Safeguarded Wallet Account* and *Customer Wallet Balances* liability; no cross-tenant netting. All bank movement is SIMULATED. | Legal / SBP e-money licensing; sponsor bank |
| D-002 | Internal loan repayment from wallet | Wallet "Key Principle" lists internal loan repayment as *ledger-only*; but a repayment converts customer money into company money, which in practice requires moving funds out of the safeguarded account. | Posting rule `loan_repayment_wallet` debits wallet liability and also moves the amount safeguarded → collection account so the safeguarded account always equals customer balances. Tenants can edit the rule (Finance › Posting rules). | Finance / safeguarding policy |
| D-003 | Cross-tenant wallet transfers | Wallet §11 forbids; no exception process defined. | Hard-blocked (`allow_cross_tenant_transfers=false`, not editable in UI). | — |
| D-004 | Islamic profit recognition | FMS §11.1 requires recognition over the tenor per Shariah standards; method (accrual vs. collection, IFAS-1/2) unspecified. | Profit / rental is recognised **when collected** (allocation of each repayment). Receivable carries principal only. Configurable via posting rules; accrual job not implemented. | Shariah board, external auditor |
| D-005 | Platform support access to tenant data | LMS §12.3: platform admin has no access to tenant client/loan data; no break-glass process defined. | Time-limited (≤24h), justified, separately granted (no self-grant), audited *support access grants* exposing operational summaries only — never customer PII. | Al-Mawakhat governance |
| D-006 | "CRO" approval stage meaning | LMS §1.2 lists stage "CRO" without expansion. | Labelled **CRO (Credit Review Officer)**; role and stage names are editable per tenant. | Al-Mawakhat credit policy |
| D-007 | Provider reversal after wallet funds were spent | Not specified. | Compensating debit is posted even if it makes the balance negative; wallet is **restricted** pending recovery and the reversal is audited. | Operations / legal |
| D-008 | Payment allocation order | Not defined. | Per product version `allocation_order`, default late amount → charges → profit → principal, oldest instalment first; excess held as *customer advance*. | Credit policy / Shariah |
| D-009 | Upfront-deducted charges on vendor-paid assets | Charges (LMS §3.5) may be "deducted upfront", but vendor disbursements pay the full asset price. | For vendor-mode products, "upfront deducted" charges are automatically spread across instalments (shown in KFS). | Product policy |
| D-010 | Late payment amounts | LMS open question; FMS §11.2 says they go to charity. | Per-day amount after grace days (cap 90 days) accrues on overdue instalments and, when collected, is credited to *Charity Fund Payable* — never income. Charity disbursements need compliance + finance approval. | Shariah board |
| D-011 | Takaful contributions | LMS §3.5 lists Takaful as a charge; payable treatment to the Takaful operator not specified. | Collected with instalment "charges" and credited to fee income; `takaful_payable` account is reserved. | Finance / Takaful operator agreement |
| D-012 | Provisioning rates and classification | FMS §4.4 references SBP/SECP prudential regulations without rates. | Configurable DPD thresholds (30/90/180/365) and rates (0/0/25/50/100%) per tenant (`lending.classification_dpd`, `lending.provisioning_rates`). | Regulator rules / auditor |
| D-013 | DPN & Affidavit e-signing | LMS §6.5: legal clearance pending. | Document types flagged `conditional` → routed to wet-ink path; staff confirm receipt. Switchable per document type. | Legal |
| D-014 | Salary disbursement for corporate clients | LMS §5.5 flags as boundary question. | Not implemented as money movement; staff loan salary deductions are recorded as repayments (channel `salary_deduction`) with reconciliation. | Stakeholders |
| D-015 | Regulatory report formats | SECP/SBP returns and Raast Annexure-A templates not supplied. | "Simulated format" reports built from live data, labelled as such. | Compliance (official templates) |
| D-016 | Staff MFA / device binding | DFS §6.4 requires MFA and device binding for internal access. | Device tracking & revocation implemented; staff OTP-MFA setting exists (`security.staff_mfa_required`) but enforcement is a known limitation. | Security review |
| D-017 | Push notifications | DFS §5.1 mentions FCM/APNs; release 1 is web-only. | In-app notifications (+ Reverb real-time); SMS fallback simulated. Push provider configured but disabled. | — |
| D-018 | Vendor portal scope | Docs require vendor-initiated origination (LMS §2.8/§4.4) but no full vendor portal spec. | Minimal vendor portal: orders, POS origination, payments, items, merchant QR (simulated), documents, profile. | Al-Mawakhat |
| D-019 | Karobar Sahulat structure (QA F1) | Product is labelled Musharakah but advertised with a fixed 26% reducing rate and cash paid to the customer's bank — reads like a conventional loan. | Demo keeps the product but labels the rate as the **expected profit rate under the agreed profit-sharing ratio** and shows the loss-sharing note; recommended production structure is Murabaha on business stock (vendor-paid). | Shariah board |
| D-020 | School Fee Financing (QA F2) | Murabaha product disbursing cash to the customer's wallet contradicts "no cash disbursed under Murabaha". | Demo pays the fee to the school as the payee (vendor-mode) instead of the wallet; Ijarah/ujrah service financing is the alternative for the Shariah board to choose. | Shariah board |
| D-021 | Murabaha accounting sequence (QA F5) | Original rule booked receivable at cost only. | Receivable now booked at contract value with deferred Murabaha profit and financed charges; profit recognised from deferred balance as instalments are collected (still collection-based, D-004). AAOIFI FAS 28 alignment to be confirmed by auditors. | Finance / auditors |
| D-022 | Tenant Admin scope (QA H7) | Template gave Tenant Admin every permission, so one person could release payouts, approve KYC and reverse journals. | Tenant Admin template now covers configuration and administration only; operational decisions require the specific roles, and payout release / journal reversal are maker-checker. | Al-Mawakhat governance |
| D-023 | Language switch in the operations portal (UI redesign) | The new reference mock-ups show `English \| اردو` in the staff top bar, but only the customer portal is fully translated and RTL. | Switch shown in ops/vendor top bars; ops screens stay left-to-right and fall back to English for untranslated strings. Full staff-side Urdu (and whether it is wanted) is open. | Al-Mawakhat |
